GitGone keeps your environment variables (API keys, database passwords, payment keys) in one place for your whole team. Everything is encrypted on your machine before it is sent: the server only stores encrypted data and can never read a value.

## How it fits together

| Part | Role |
|---|---|
| **Server** | Stores the encrypted secrets, the users, teams and roles, and the audit log. Open source, runs with Docker. |
| **CLI** | Encrypts, sends and gets your secrets from the terminal. It is where all the encryption happens. |
| **GitGone Cloud** | Hosts a private server for your organization, with a web console, billing and support. |

You use the same CLI and the same server whether you self-host or use GitGone Cloud.

## What you get

- **End-to-end encryption**: secrets are encrypted with keys the server never has.
- **Access down to the environment**: roles decide who can read or push each environment, and production can be protected.
- **History and rollback**: every push is a new version you can restore.
- **Clean departures**: removing someone rotates the keys they knew.
- **CI ready**: tokens limited to one environment let your pipelines read their secrets.
- **Audit log**: who pulled, pushed or shared what, and when.
