---
title: Introduction
description: The GitGone command-line tool, to share your team's secrets from the terminal.
---

The GitGone CLI encrypts your `.env` on your machine, sends it to your GitGone server, and gets it back for your teammates. The server only ever stores encrypted data.

With the CLI you can:

- **Sync secrets** between your `.env` and the server, per environment (`development`, `staging`, `production`…).
- **Run your app** with the secrets in its environment, without writing them to disk.
- **Share access** with teammates and give them roles, down to a single environment.
- **Keep a history** of every version, and roll back in one command.

## Quickstart

```bash
npm install -g @project-gitgone/cli
gitgone
```

Run alone, `gitgone` guides you: it asks for your server URL, signs you in, links the folder to a project, then offers the everyday actions.

<Callout type="info">
  Some commands were renamed. If you used `gitgone keys`, `gitgone passwd` or `gitgone admin`, see the [migration guide](/docs/cli/migration).
</Callout>
